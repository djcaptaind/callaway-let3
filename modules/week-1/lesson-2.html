Our Learning Environment

The Standard

The standard is not perfection. The standard is consistent effort, respect, responsibility, and improvement.
Be PreparedBring required materials, charge your device, and check Canvas.
Be RespectfulListen when others speak and protect every person’s right to learn.
Be EngagedParticipate, ask questions, and complete the assigned learning.
Be AccountableOwn your choices, meet deadlines, and correct mistakes.
Be SafeFollow instructions and use classroom equipment appropriately.
Be a LeaderModel the conduct you expect from others, even without a title.
Daily Success

Classroom Routines

  • 1Enter ready: report on time, begin the posted task, and limit unnecessary distractions.
  • 2Check directions: review the board, Canvas, or instructor guidance before asking what to do.
  • 3Participate professionally: contribute without interrupting, distracting, or disrespecting others.
  • 4Protect learning time: keep phones and unrelated materials away unless authorized.
  • 5Close out correctly: submit work, return materials, organize the area, and note upcoming requirements.

3. A cadet misses a deadline, accepts responsibility, communicates with the instructor, and completes the work.

5. A cadet respectfully asks for clarification after first reviewing the directions.
