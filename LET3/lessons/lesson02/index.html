LET 3 • WEEK 2 • LESSON 2

Decisions Define Leaders.

Cadets examine how leadership decisions influence individuals and teams. Through realistic scenarios, video analysis, and reflection, cadets evaluate consequences and practice making ethical, informed, and timely decisions.

Analyze

Identify the problem, facts, stakeholders, and values involved.

Evaluate

Compare choices, risks, consequences, and effects on trust.

Decide

Select and defend a responsible course of action.

ESSENTIAL QUESTION

How do the decisions leaders make shape trust, performance, and the success of a team?

LEARNING OBJECTIVE

Analyze leadership situations, evaluate possible courses of action, and select decisions that demonstrate responsibility, integrity, accountability, and sound judgment.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the End of the Lesson

  • Analyze leadership situations using critical thinking.
  • Identify ethical and unethical leadership decisions.
  • Evaluate short- and long-term consequences.
  • Explain how communication supports decision-making.
  • Apply leadership values to realistic scenarios.
  • Defend decisions using evidence and reasoning.
