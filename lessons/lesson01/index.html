FOUNDATIONAL LEADERSHIP

Leadership Is a Choice.

Before anyone gives you a title, rank, or position, you decide whether you will step forward, take responsibility, and influence what happens next.

45–60 minutes Interactive lesson Progress saved
ESSENTIAL QUESTION

What does it mean to choose to lead when no one is watching?

PART 1

Leadership Starts Before the Title

Leadership is not limited to commanders, captains, teachers, or adults. It begins whenever a person chooses to improve a situation, help someone succeed, or accept responsibility.

01

Choice

You decide whether to act, speak up, help, prepare, or take ownership.

02

Influence

Your attitude and behavior affect what others believe is normal and acceptable.

03

Responsibility

Leaders do not wait for excuses to disappear before taking the next right action.

04

Service

Strong leadership improves the team, not only the leader's reputation.

KEY IDEA

Leadership is visible in the small choices people make consistently.
